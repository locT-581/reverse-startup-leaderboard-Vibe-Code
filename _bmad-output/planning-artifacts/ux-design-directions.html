UX Design Directions Explorer

1. The Corporate Facade

Deceptively boring, stark, and standard. Uses Arial and tight borders. Makes the upcoming chaotic anti-UX elements completely unexpected and jarring.

2. Hyper-Modern SaaS

Soft gradients, glassmorphism, rounded corners. Looks like a highly funded, premium startup. The contrast between this beauty and the hostile UI logic creates maximum irony.

3. Troll Capitalist (Store)

Elite dark mode, neon gradients, high contrast. Designed to make spending money feel incredibly powerful, frictionless, and exclusive.

4. Mercy Mode

Oversized, rounded, condescendingly playful with high-saturation colors. Activates as a fallback when the user fails anti-UX challenges too many times.
